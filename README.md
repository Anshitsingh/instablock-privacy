# Instablock – Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 12, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Instablock is a digital wellbeing application designed to help users reduce distractions, manage screen time and block distracting content such as Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

We respect your privacy and aim to process information locally on your device.

## 2. Permissions and Information Accessed

Instablock requests the following Android permissions and special access:

**Accessibility Service**

Used to identify foreground applications and inspect relevant user-interface elements to detect distracting content, including Reels and Shorts. This information is processed locally to enforce blocking rules.

Instablock does not use this capability to collect or transmit private messages, passwords or personal communications.

**Usage Access**

Used to measure time spent in individual applications, calculate usage statistics and enforce user-configured daily time limits.

**Display Over Other Apps**

Used to display blocking screens and digital wellbeing interventions over other applications.

**Foreground Service and Background Operation**

Used to maintain protection, monitor configured restrictions and restore functionality when necessary.

**Notifications**

Used to communicate protection status and relevant alerts.

**Boot Completed Access**

Used to resume protection after a device restart.

## 3. Data Collection and Sharing

Based on the application's current implementation:

- Accessibility information is processed locally.
- Application usage information is used for screen-time tracking and blocking.
- Instablock does not intentionally transmit accessibility content or application usage history to external servers.
- We do not sell personal information.
- We do not share personal information with advertisers.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Application settings, blocking rules and relevant usage information are managed on the user's device.

Users should protect their devices with appropriate security measures.

## 5. User Control

Users can manage or revoke Android permissions through their device settings.

Revoking Accessibility access disables functionality that depends on detecting distracting application screens.

Revoking Usage Access disables accurate application usage tracking and associated time-budget enforcement.

Revoking overlay access may prevent blocking screens from appearing over other applications.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Instablock is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application functionality or privacy practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page.
